Multi-Cloud Migration & Utilities

Take the application plans and move them: landing zones, waves, replication, test, cutover, rollback, DNS, validation, decommission and a tracking board. Then keep changing the estate with utilities (a server, a disk, a port, a DNS record, a patch run), each a small Terraform and Ansible bundle. The plan is shared with Application Migration.
