Multi-cloud decision matrix

Not "which cloud is best" — nobody can answer that. Given these constraints, which of the five is left, and why. Every rule states what it looked at, which way it pushed and where the claim came from, so any of them can be read and disagreed with on its own. Region coverage, sovereign offerings and pricing change constantly and cannot be checked from an offline toolkit, so those are reported rather than scored.
